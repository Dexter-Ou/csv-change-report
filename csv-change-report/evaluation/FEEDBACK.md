# Voluntary feedback prompts

Use general descriptions or wholly invented tiny data. Keep all real exports,
column names, hashes, paths, personal/customer information and secrets private.

- Version and Python/OS version (omit identifying machine details):
- Did you try supplied synthetic samples or your own local copies?
- What decision did you hope the report would help you make?
- Could you distinguish changed, unchanged and failed comparisons?
- What was confusing or missing? What did you do instead?
- Would you choose to use it again? Why or why not?
- Optional: what would have to improve before you would consider paying?

Do not promise or send payment. Interest is not a purchase. Participation is
optional; no telemetry or automatic submission is included.

Optional: Did explicit exclusions make the review clearer? Could you tell that
excluded fields still differed? Describe generally; do not paste private data.
